# Best Time To Visit (sample knowledge base doc)

## Goa, India
Best visited November to February (cool, dry season). Avoid June-September
(monsoon) unless you specifically want the lush, quiet, off-season vibe and
don't mind rain disrupting beach plans.

## Kerala, India
October to March offers pleasant weather. The monsoon (June-September) is
popular for Ayurvedic treatments and houseboat backwater trips at a
discount, but expect heavy rain.

## Japan
Late March to April (cherry blossoms) and October-November (autumn foliage)
are the most popular and most crowded. Winter (Dec-Feb) is good for skiing
in Hokkaido/Nagano and generally has fewer tourists.

## Southeast Asia (Thailand, Vietnam)
November to February is dry season across most of the region and the most
comfortable for sightseeing. April-May can be extremely hot before monsoon
season begins.

## Europe
May-June and September are considered shoulder season: good weather, fewer
crowds and lower prices than the July-August peak summer season.
