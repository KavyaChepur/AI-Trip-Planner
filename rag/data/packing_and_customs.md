# Packing & Local Customs (sample knowledge base doc)

## Beach destinations (Goa, Bali, Thailand)
Pack light breathable cotton/linen clothing, reef-safe sunscreen, a
waterproof phone pouch, and modest cover-up clothing for visiting temples
(shoulders and knees covered).

## Temple / religious site etiquette (India, Thailand, Japan)
Remove shoes before entering temples and many homes. Dress modestly -
avoid sleeveless tops and shorts. Some sites prohibit photography inside
the main shrine area; look for posted signage.

## Cold weather destinations (Japan winter, Europe winter)
Layered clothing works best: thermal base layer, insulating mid-layer,
waterproof/windproof outer shell. Don't forget gloves and a warm hat -
most heat loss is through extremities and the head.

## Money & tipping norms
- Japan: tipping is not customary and can be seen as awkward.
- USA: 15-20% tipping is expected at sit-down restaurants.
- India/Southeast Asia: tipping is appreciated but not mandatory; small
  amounts (5-10%) are common in tourist-facing restaurants.

## Local transport etiquette
In Japan, phone calls on trains are discouraged; keep voices low. In many
European cities, validate paper transit tickets before boarding to avoid
fines.
