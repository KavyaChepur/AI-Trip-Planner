## India
Most foreign nationals require an e-Visa or regular visa to enter India.
Indian citizens do not require a visa for domestic travel. Passport validity
of at least 6 months beyond the date of entry is commonly required.

## Schengen Area (Europe)
Many nationalities require a Schengen visa for short stays (up to 90 days in
any 180-day period) covering most EU countries. Some nationalities are
visa-exempt. Travel insurance covering at least EUR 30,000 is typically
required as part of the visa application.

## Southeast Asia
Thailand, Vietnam, Indonesia, and Malaysia offer visa-on-arrival or
visa-exempt entry for many nationalities for short tourist stays (commonly
15-30 days), though rules vary by passport and change periodically.

## General tips
- Always verify current visa requirements on the destination country's
  official immigration website close to your travel date - rules change.
- Keep both digital and printed copies of visa approvals, hotel bookings,
  and return tickets when required at immigration.
- Some countries require proof of onward travel and sufficient funds.
